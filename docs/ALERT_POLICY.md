# Alert Policy

## Priority levels

P0 — Immediate
- exploit/security incident
- major liquidity failure
- abnormal high-confidence wallet activity
- exceptional new launch signal

P1 — High
- strong early opportunity
- significant narrative acceleration
- major protocol/capital-flow change

P2 — Watch
- interesting early signal needing confirmation

P3 — Research
- useful context but not alert-worthy

## Quality gate

A P0/P1 alert should normally include at least two independent evidence classes:

- on-chain
- market
- social
- catalyst/news
- wallet behavior

Exceptions must be explicitly marked.

## Anti-spam

Deduplicate by:
- asset
- event
- time window
- underlying cause

If an existing alert materially changes, update it rather than creating another duplicate.

## Language

Use concise, factual language.

Never promise profit.

Never state that an asset will rise.

State:
- what was observed
- when it was observed
- what evidence supports it
- what could invalidate it
- what risks remain
